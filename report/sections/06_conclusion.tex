\section{Conclusion}

We built a question-answering agent over SEC filings in which the LLM only reads the question and
phrases the answer, while SQL, search and code find every number, compute every change and check
every citation. On AWS, a Spark pipeline on one EC2 instance loaded the figures and 10-Q text of
every SEC filer since 2019: 9,695 companies, 140,436 10-Qs, 3.0 million figures and 4.55 million
passages, for about \$20.

The experiments show three things. First, the workflow is much more accurate than simple RAG on
quarterly numbers (96\% against 70\% with Claude Haiku 5.5), and the gap is largest for small local
models (84\% against 38\% with Qwen2.5-7B on a laptop). A closed-book baseline confirms that
retrieval is needed at all: without it Haiku answers only 30\% of our questions and 23\% of
FinanceBench, with no source a reader can check. Second, on the external FinanceBench
benchmark the workflow is ahead of simple RAG in every question type with Haiku (69\% against 56\%)
and gives fewer wrong answers, but its structured answer format is too demanding for small models.
Third, our own layer is fast (about 10 ms per question, more than 200 questions per second on one
process), and the Spark jobs scale to the physical cores of the machine, so the LLM and the SEC
download limit are the only real bottlenecks.

\paragraph{Limitations.} About 27\% of 10-Qs have no MD\&A heading that our section detector
recognises, so they have no text. Quarters are calendar quarters, so for companies with a different
fiscal year ``Q2'' means April to June. Passages from 10-Q HTML leave out tables, so ratio questions
on bulk 10-Qs usually decline. Document QA checks that the inputs of a calculation are printed in the
filing, but not that they are the right lines. Free-text grades come from an LLM grader that agreed
with a stronger model on 90\% of a sample; a check by a person is still to do.

\paragraph{Next steps.} Before the final report we plan to (1) check the grader sample by hand,
(2) give small models a simpler document QA format, (3) run the load test on smaller instance types
(t3.large, c7i.xlarge) and with several API workers behind a load balancer, and (4) improve the
MD\&A detector and keep tables in 10-Q passages.
